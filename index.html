I finished a B.Sc. in Computer Science & AI at McGill in May 2026 and started at SS&C Algorithmics that July. Most of my work so far has sat between software and finance: risk tooling, portfolio analytics, and moving data out of spreadsheets and old files into databases people can actually query.

At SS&C I work on the backend of ARIA, a Java assistant that risk analysts use to ask questions about their portfolios. On the side, I handle the engineering at Aspequity, a small company that sells cash flow and NAV forecasts for private equity funds.
