About

I'm a software engineer working where AI, data engineering, and quantitative finance meet. At SS&C Algorithmics I build ARIA, an enterprise Java AI assistant for financial risk analysis. In parallel, I co-founded Aspequity, where I lead engineering on a C++ platform that forecasts private equity cash flows and valuations for institutional clients.

Before that, I led Santander's first LLM audit and built portfolio and risk analytics at Northfield. I graduated from McGill in 2026 with a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence and minors in Mathematics and Finance. I like systems that need to be fast, auditable, and right.
